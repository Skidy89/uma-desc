About

Gold Ship is a Japanese Thoroughbred racehorse and a character from the multimedia franchise "Uma Musume Pretty Derby." She is known for her unique personality, often described as lazy and carefree, preferring to avoid training and races. Despite her laid-back attitude, Gold Ship has a strong racing pedigree and has achieved significant success on the track. In the "Uma Musume" series, she is portrayed as a charming and endearing character, beloved by fans for her quirky behavior and memorable moments.
